# Scope and Personas

## Primary personas

| Persona | Key needs |
|---|---|
| Customer | Discover, customise, order, pay, track and receive support |
| Cashier | Enter orders quickly, accept payments and resolve issues |
| Kitchen staff | Receive accurate tickets and manage preparation queues |
| Manager | Control menus, shifts, discounts, refunds and daily closing |
| Owner | Monitor sales, margin, staff, stock and branch performance |
| Driver | Accept jobs, navigate, update status and prove delivery |
| Platform operator | Onboard tenants, manage plans, support and monitor integrations |

## Scope model

- **MVP:** ordering, payments, administration, KDS, notifications and basic reporting.
- **Growth:** inventory, CRM, loyalty, reservations, delivery and POS adapters.
- **Intelligence:** recommendations, forecasting, voice ordering and operational insights.
- **Enterprise:** franchises, central kitchen, advanced permissions, data export and dedicated tenancy.
