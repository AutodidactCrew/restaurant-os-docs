# Customer and Ordering

## Identity and access
- Guest ordering
- Email/mobile registration
- OTP verification
- Apple and Google sign-in
- Saved addresses and payment preferences
- Privacy and marketing consent

## Ordering journeys
- QR dine-in ordering
- Counter-assisted ordering
- Pickup and curbside pickup
- Restaurant-managed delivery
- Third-party delivery
- Scheduled and recurring catering orders
- Group ordering and shared carts

## Cart and checkout
- Variants, modifiers and preparation notes
- Allergy and dietary declarations
- Coupons, loyalty and gift cards
- Tax, service charge, tips and delivery fees
- Split bill and split tender
- Order confirmation and live status
- Cancellation and refund eligibility rules
