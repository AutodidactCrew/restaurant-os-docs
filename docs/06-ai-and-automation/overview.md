# AI and Automation Strategy

## Provider options
| Option | Strength | Use consideration |
|---|---|---|
| OpenAI | Realtime, multimodal and broad developer ecosystem | Primary conversational and voice use |
| Anthropic | Long-context analysis and writing | Business insights and document reasoning |
| Gemini | Google ecosystem and multimodal capability | Maps/Workspace-aligned deployments |
| Self-hosted models | Control and customisation | Higher operational burden |

## AI use cases
- Menu discovery and natural-language ordering
- Contextual upsell within restaurant-defined constraints
- Review and feedback summarisation
- Demand and inventory forecasting
- Fraud/anomaly detection
- Phone and voice ordering
- Operator copilot for reports and actions

## Guardrails
- AI cannot invent menu items, prices or allergens.
- Business rules and structured data remain authoritative.
- High-risk actions require human confirmation.
- Prompts and outputs must be logged with privacy controls.
